Research

Starting in 2026, I have been working on research into computational altruism. Particularly, I have been studying the emergent behaviors by which machine learning models in community networks can become altruistic (or selfish) based on the behaviors of the models around them. Paper on this upcoming!

In 2024, I was active in research with quantum computing, in the areas of variational quantum algorithms and approaches to simultaneous solving processes. The idea is that one can observe the solution process on one problem instance to get some idea of how to approach another problem instance, so I (and the others in the Kent-Case Quantum Group) try to do just that. My very first paper, which you can read here, was accepted and presented at at QCCC-24 in June 2024. This was joint work with Eric Yarnot, with help and contributions from several others whose names are in the PDF.

I started to get seriously involved with programming in late 2019, when I was a tenth-grader at Davis Senior High. Since then, I have developed projects in many languages:

  • I have written many projects in C. In particular, Y-flat and cml, respectively a programming language and a machine learning model both from scratch (see below).
  • I have worked extensively with Python. I did my 2025 summer internship project (see below) almost exclusively in it, and it is also my language of choice for running simulations in my ML research.
  • Java is a language I used intensively in 2020 and 2021. Particularly, QME (see below) is a project I managed in Java. A lot of my early forays into gamedev were done in Java.
  • I used C++ to create much of my later gamedev work, particularly Minecraft3D (see below) where I created a game engine from scratch.
  • I took, and later TA'd for, a course where the main assignments were all completed in Scheme. As such, I have some experience with Lisps, particularly in the techniques of tail recursion and continuation passing.
  • I know the rudiments of Go and Rust, which I respectively used to complete my computer science senior project and a Minecraft clone in 2026.
  • Webdev tools: I know enough HTML to format this page. (I made the CSS entirely with AI.)

While the full extent of my projects and tools is available on my GitHub page (both my own repositories and the organizations I am part of), here is a rundown of some of the highlights:

  • cml (Summer 2023): from-scratch machine-learning framework in C.
  • Y-flat (2022): incomplete compiler (with relating tooling done by Jake Roggenbuck among others) for my custom programming language Y-flat. Several thousand lines of C code, with a lexer, parser, analyzer, etc. all custom-made.
  • Minecraft clone (2021): a sandbox world generator, modeled and textured after Minecraft, that I made using OpenGL and C++.
  • QME (2021): an (incomplete) 4X strategy game whose development I led/co-led, collaboratively with several programmers, artists, and designers. This was a wonderful experience in collaborative development and teamwork.
